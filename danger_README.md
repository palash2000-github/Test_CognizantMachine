## Notes

- Uses only the Python standard library (`tkinter`, `random`, `datetime`).
- Enter `day`, `month`, and `year` separately.
- Years from `1920` to `2050` get a year-specific fortune.
- Years outside that range show a funny age-related message.
- Press `Enter` to reveal a fortune and `Esc` to clear the form.
